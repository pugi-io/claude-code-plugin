name: start-from-template
description: Find a Pugi starter site or app in the public catalogue to start a build from.
---

Use `catalog_search` when the user wants ideas, examples or a starting point for a site or app ("show me café websites", "a template for a dental clinic"). It is free and read-only. Search with the user's business type and style in plain words, show the few best matches with their preview image and one-line summary, and let the user pick. When they pick one, include its name and what they liked in the brief you pass to `build_site`. Do not claim a template is the user's own site.
